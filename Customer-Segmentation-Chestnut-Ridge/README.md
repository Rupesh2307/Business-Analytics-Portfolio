# Customer Segmentation Analysis — Chestnut Ridge

### 📊 Overview
This project is a comprehensive customer segmentation study conducted for Chestnut Ridge, aiming to identify distinct customer groups based on their preferences, behaviors, and demographics. The insights generated support targeted marketing strategies and help improve business performance and customer satisfaction.

### 🎯 Objective
To apply clustering techniques on customer data to uncover meaningful customer segments and provide actionable marketing insights.

### 🧰 Tools & Techniques
- Tools: R  
- Techniques: Hierarchical Clustering, K-Means Clustering, Data Profiling  
- Data: Customer preferences (choice variety, service quality, low prices) and demographics (age, income)

### 💡 Key Insights
- Identified multiple distinct customer segments based on demographic and preference variables  
- Each segment shows unique buying behavior and sensitivity to price, variety, or service  
- Recommended targeted marketing strategies to align offerings with the most profitable segments
