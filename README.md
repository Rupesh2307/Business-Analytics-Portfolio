## Projects

### 1. Multi-Echelon Inventory Simulation (MSc Dissertation)
**Application of Business Analytics in Supply Chain and Operations Management Using Simulation**

A simulation-based analytics project examining the efficiency–resilience trade-off in multi-echelon inventory systems under demand and supply-side disruptions.

**Key focus:**
- Three-echelon inventory modelling (supplier–DC–retail)
- Real-world retail demand data (Walmart)
- Demand shocks, supply lead-time disruptions, and compound risk scenarios
- Cost–resilience trade-off analysis using safety stock sweeps
- Managerial decision-support visualisations

**Tools:** Python, simulation modelling, data analysis
